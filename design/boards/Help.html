How a label is checked

  1. Read. The image is read with local OCR (Tesseract), twice, so large brand lines and short centred lines are both caught. Nothing leaves this server.
  2. Find each field. For every value on the application we look for the best-matching text on the label, so we know where it was read.
  3. Compare. Capitalization, punctuation, spacing and unit spelling are ignored ("Alc./Vol." = "ABV", "750 mL" = "750ml", 90 proof = 45%). Alcohol and net contents are compared as numbers.
  4. Government warning. Must be present, word for word, with "GOVERNMENT WARNING:" in capitals and bold.
What the results mean
MATCHIdentical after normalization. NEAR MATCHSame words with different capitalization or punctuation (brand name), or at least 88% similar. You decide. MISMATCHThe label says something different from the application. NOT FOUNDNothing on the label resembles the application value. SKIPPEDNot on the application, so not checked.
PASSEvery field matches and the warning is correct. REVIEWAt least one NEAR MATCH or a doubtful warning check. Needs your look, usually seconds. FAILA MISMATCH, a NOT FOUND, or a warning that is missing, misworded or not in capitals.
Government warning checks
PresentThe statement was found on the label. WordingCompared word for word with 27 CFR 16.21; differences are listed. 97% similar or more is flagged for review (it may be a reading error); anything bigger fails. Capitals"GOVERNMENT WARNING:" must be printed in capitals. BoldEstimated from the thickness of the heading's letter strokes against the rest of the statement (bold from 1.30× thicker). A heuristic, so a doubtful result asks for a look rather than failing the label.
The thresholds, in one place
Text fields: NEAR MATCH from88 / 100 Text fields: NOT FOUND below60 / 100 Alcohol content tolerance0.05 points Net contents tolerance0.5 mL Warning wording: review from97 / 100 Heading bold from1.30×

Good to know
  • Works best with flat, straight-on label images (artwork files or scans). Photos at an angle, with glare or poor lighting are out of scope for this prototype.
  • Images up to 20 MB; batches up to 500 labels. Nothing is stored after the check.
  • Required text: "GOVERNMENT WARNING: (1) According to the Surgeon General, women should not drink alcoholic beverages during pregnancy because of the risk of birth defects. (2) Consumption of alcoholic beverages impairs your ability to drive a car or operate machinery, and may cause health problems."
