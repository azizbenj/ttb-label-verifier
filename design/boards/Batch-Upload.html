Verify a label against its COLA (Certificate of Label Approval) application.

Check many labels at once

One CSV row per application, plus the label images. Images are matched to rows by file name.

What you get back

1
A triage count first.
How many need you (REVIEW), how many are blocked (FAIL), how many are done (PASS).
2
A review queue you can step through.
Label and evidence side by side, one decision per item, J/K to move.
3
Exports that match the queue.
CSV of everything, or just the passes, or just the fails with their reasons, including your decisions.

Results start appearing while the batch is still running, so you can begin on the review pile straight away. 250 labels take about 90 seconds; 0.3 s per label.
