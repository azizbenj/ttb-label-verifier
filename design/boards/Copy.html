Every headline, label, helper and message

Same register as the current app: plain, second person, one idea per sentence, no exclamation marks, no jargon the agents did not already use. The verdict words are fixed by the regulation and the team and never paraphrased: MATCH, NEAR MATCH, MISMATCH, NOT FOUND, SKIPPED, PASS, REVIEW, FAIL, "government warning". Per-field reasons come from the pipeline unchanged. Strings in grey mono are where they live.
